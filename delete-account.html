Delete your Tija account

Tija works without an account. If you created one to sync between devices, you can delete it and everything stored with it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Tija and go to Profile.
  2. In Cloud Sync, under Account, tap Delete Account.
  3. Enter your password. Choose whether to also erase the notes on that device, then tap Delete Account.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email us from the address you signed up with and we'll delete the account for you within 7 days.

Email a deletion request

We'll only act on requests sent from the account's own email address, and we'll reply to confirm once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address and password
  • Your profile: name, username and profile picture
  • Everything you synced: tasks, notes, folders, templates and reminders
  • Files you attached to tasks
  • AI assistant data: usage counts and search indexes of your notes
  • Access tokens for AI apps you connected (such as Claude Desktop or Cursor)

This can't be undone. Deleted data can't be recovered, and signing up again creates a new, empty account.

What isn't deleted

  • Notes saved on your own devices stay there unless you choose to erase them in the app, or uninstall Tija.
  • Copies in our hosting provider's routine backups are removed when those backups expire.
