Tija Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026 · What changed

In short: Tija works on your device first. Without an account, your notes never leave your device. If you create an account, your notes are stored on our servers so they sync between your devices. The AI assistant is off until you turn it on, and you choose where it runs. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.

This policy explains what the Tija app ("Tija", "we", "us") collects, why, who else processes it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Tija app on every platform it's available on (Android and Windows, and iOS and macOS once released) and to this website.

1. Using Tija without an account

Everything you create (tasks, notes, folders, templates, reminders and attachments) is stored only on your device. We don't receive it. A few optional features still contact other services from your device; they are listed in section 4.

Where it's stored on your device

  • Android, iPhone and iPad: in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. Uninstalling Tija removes it.
  • Windows: in your user account's app data folder, %LOCALAPPDATA%\Tija (the database, attachments and profile pictures), inside your own Windows user profile.
  • macOS: in the app's own container in your user account.

2. If you create an account

An account is optional. It lets you back up your work and sync it across devices. With an account we store:

  • Account details: your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Profile: your name, username and, if you add one, a profile picture.
  • Your content: tasks, notes, folders, templates, reminders and files you attach, so they sync between your devices.
  • Sync records: which device made a change and when, so edits from different devices merge correctly.

This data is stored with Supabase, our hosting provider, in the European Union (Ireland). It's encrypted in transit and access is limited to your account.

3. The AI assistant

The AI assistant is optional and off by default. When you use it, the text it needs for that request (for example the note you asked it to summarize) is sent to the AI you chose in Profile → AI Assistant:

ChoiceWhere your text goes
On this device Nowhere. The AI built into your device (Apple Intelligence on iPhone, iPad and Mac, or Gemini Nano on supported Android phones), or a model you download, runs entirely on the device. On Windows, only downloaded models are available. Models are downloaded from Hugging Face.
Tija Cloud (needs an account) Our server, which sends it to Anthropic (Claude) to generate the answer. We keep a count of your requests per day to apply the daily allowance, and the number of tokens they used; we don't store your prompts or the answers.
Your own model Directly from your device to the server or provider you set up (for example Ollama or LM Studio on your network, or OpenAI, Google Gemini, Mistral, Groq or OpenRouter with your own API key). Their own privacy policies apply. Your API key is stored encrypted on your device and is never sent to us.

If your chosen AI fails

With "Your own model" or "On this device", you can turn on If it fails, use Claude. When it's on and your chosen model can't complete a request, the same request is sent again to Tija Cloud and handled as described in the Tija Cloud row above, so it reaches our server and Anthropic. This needs an account and counts against your daily allowance.

The setting is on by default when you connect a provider with your own API key, and off by default for Ollama, LM Studio and on-device models. You can change it at any time in Profile → AI Assistant.

Search by meaning and "Ask Tija"

With an account and the AI assistant turned on, Tija can search your notes by meaning. To do this, our server converts your synced notes into search indexes (lists of numbers that represent meaning), and your search is converted the same way. English text is processed on our own servers; notes and searches in other languages may be sent to Voyage AI for this step. The indexes are stored with your account and deleted with it. Notes kept only on your device aren't indexed.

Connected AI apps

You can let other AI apps, such as Claude Desktop, Claude Code or Cursor, read (and, if you allow it, change) your synced notes, using an access token you create in Tija. Those apps then handle your notes under their own privacy policies. You can disconnect an app at any time in Profile → AI Assistant → Connect other AI apps.

Reporting AI responses

If you report an AI response, we receive the response, the reason you chose, any comment you add, and which AI model was used. We use reports to find and fix harmful or incorrect output.

4. Other features that contact outside services

  • Link icons: to show an icon for a link you save, the link's domain name (for example example.com, not the full address) is sent to Google's favicon service.
  • Link summaries: the page is downloaded directly by your device, then summarized by the AI you chose.
  • Bug reports and feedback: if you send one, we receive what you write, the email address you enter and, if you add them, screenshots and device details.
  • Reminders and the morning brief are created on your device and send nothing to us.
  • This website loads nothing from other services and sets no cookies. If you switch between light and dark, that choice is saved only in your browser.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We don't use your notes to train AI models.

6. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: account, database, file storage and server functions (EU, Ireland).
  • Anthropic: generates AI answers for Tija Cloud, including requests sent there by the Claude fallback.
  • Voyage AI: creates search indexes for notes that aren't in English.
  • Brevo: sends account emails (sign-up confirmation and password reset codes) to your email address.
  • Google: email delivery for support messages and reports; link icons.

They process data only to provide these services to us. Services you connect yourself (your own AI model or provider, and connected AI apps) are chosen by you and act under their own terms.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account data and synced content: until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Daily AI request counts and the tokens they used: kept with your account.
  • Items you delete (notes, folders, templates): kept on your device for 30 days in Recently deleted, so you can restore them, and then removed. A copy marked as deleted stays in your account so your other devices can follow the delete.
  • A deleted account: scheduled for deletion and removed for good after 7 days (see Delete your account).
  • Tester access: if you enter a tester code, we keep a record that you used it and when your testing access ends, until you delete your account.
  • Support emails and reports: as long as needed to handle them.
  • Copies in our hosting provider's routine backups are removed when those backups expire.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Use Tija without an account, or turn the AI assistant off at any time.
  • Edit or delete any item; deletes sync to your other devices.
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Cloud & Sync → Delete Account) or by request. The account and all data stored with it are permanently deleted after 7 days; sign in again within that time to keep it.
  • Restore deleted items for 30 days in Profile → Data and Storage → Recently deleted.
  • Access or correct your data, or ask a question about it, by emailing us.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or Kenya), you may have further rights under data protection law, including to object to processing or to complain to your data protection authority.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit between the app and our servers. Each account can only access its own data. Access tokens for connected AI apps are stored only as secure hashes. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

10. Children

Tija isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when our practices change and update the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.

October 17, 2026: deleting an account now takes 7 days and can be cancelled by signing in; deleted items can be restored for 30 days; we describe the AI token counts we keep and tester codes.

October 15, 2026: the policy now covers every platform, says where data is stored on each device, and describes the Claude fallback for your own and on-device models.
