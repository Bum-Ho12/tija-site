Tija Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

In short: Tija works on your device first. Without an account, your notes never leave your phone. If you create an account, your notes are stored on our servers so they sync between your devices. The AI assistant is off until you turn it on, and you choose where it runs. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.

This policy explains what the Tija app ("Tija", "we", "us") collects, why, who else processes it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Tija app on Android and the websites linked from it.

1. Using Tija without an account

Everything you create (tasks, notes, folders, templates, reminders and attachments) is stored only on your device. We don't receive it. A few optional features still contact other services from your device; they are listed in section 4.

2. If you create an account

An account is optional. It lets you back up your work and sync it across devices. With an account we store:

  • Account details: your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Profile: your name, username and, if you add one, a profile picture.
  • Your content: tasks, notes, folders, templates, reminders and files you attach, so they sync between your devices.
  • Sync records: which device made a change and when, so edits from different devices merge correctly.

This data is stored with Supabase, our hosting provider, in the European Union (Ireland). It's encrypted in transit and access is limited to your account.

3. The AI assistant

The AI assistant is optional and off by default. When you use it, the text it needs for that request (for example the note you asked it to summarize) is sent to the AI you chose in Settings → AI:

ChoiceWhere your text goes
On this device Nowhere. The AI built into your phone, or a model you download, runs entirely on the device. Models are downloaded from Hugging Face.
Tija Cloud (needs an account) Our server, which sends it to Anthropic (Claude) to generate the answer. We keep a count of your requests per day to apply the daily allowance; we don't store your prompts or the answers.
Your own model Directly from your device to the server or provider you set up (for example Ollama or LM Studio on your network, or OpenAI, Google Gemini, Mistral, Groq or OpenRouter with your own API key). Their own privacy policies apply. Your API key is stored encrypted on your device and is never sent to us.

Search by meaning and "Ask Tija"

With an account and the AI assistant turned on, Tija can search your notes by meaning. To do this, our server converts your synced notes into search indexes (lists of numbers that represent meaning), and your search is converted the same way. English text is processed on our own servers; notes and searches in other languages may be sent to Voyage AI for this step. The indexes are stored with your account and deleted with it.

Connected AI apps

You can let other AI apps, such as Claude Desktop, Claude Code or Cursor, read (and, if you allow it, change) your synced notes, using an access token you create in Tija. Those apps then handle your notes under their own privacy policies. You can revoke a token at any time in Settings → AI.

Reporting AI responses

If you report an AI response, we receive the response, the reason you chose, any comment you add, and which AI model was used. We use reports to find and fix harmful or incorrect output.

4. Other features that contact outside services

  • Link icons: to show an icon for a link you save, the link's domain name (for example example.com, not the full address) is sent to Google's favicon service.
  • Link summaries: the page is downloaded directly by your device, then summarized by the AI you chose.
  • Bug reports and feedback: if you send one, we receive what you write, the email address you enter and, if you add them, screenshots and device details.
  • Reminders and the morning brief are created on your device and send nothing to us.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We don't use your notes to train AI models.

6. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: account, database, file storage and server functions (EU, Ireland).
  • Anthropic: generates AI answers for Tija Cloud.
  • Voyage AI: creates search indexes for notes that aren't in English.
  • Google: email delivery for support messages and reports; link icons.

They process data only to provide these services to us. Services you connect yourself (your own AI model or provider, and connected AI apps) are chosen by you and act under their own terms.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account data and synced content: until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Daily AI request counts: kept with your account.
  • Support emails and reports: as long as needed to handle them.
  • Copies in our hosting provider's routine backups are removed when those backups expire.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Use Tija without an account, or turn the AI assistant off at any time.
  • Edit or delete any item; deletes sync to your other devices.
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Cloud Sync → Delete Account) or by request. This permanently deletes your account and all data stored with it.
  • Access or correct your data, or ask a question about it, by emailing us.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or Kenya), you may have further rights under data protection law, including to object to processing or to complain to your data protection authority.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit between the app and our servers. Each account can only access its own data. Access tokens for connected AI apps are stored only as secure hashes. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

10. Children

Tija isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when our practices change and update the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
