Everything in one place

Tija mixes notes and tasks, so a plan can start as a thought and end as a checklist with a due date.

Write your way

Mix text, lists and checklists, code, tables and images in a note. The formatting bar adds bold, highlights and links as you type.

Plan & track

Turn notes into tasks, give them due dates and see them on the calendar. Start from templates and export as PDF or Markdown.

Meet your assistant

Capture a task in plain words, plan your day and ask questions about your notes. Nothing is saved until you accept it.

On every device

Tija works offline first. Sign in to back up your work and pick up where you left off on any device.

Stay on track

Reminders reach you even when Tija is in the background, plus a morning brief and a home-screen widget on Android.

Private by design

No account needed, no ads. Without an account your notes never leave your device, and usage statistics stay off unless you say yes.

Your AI, your choice

The assistant is off until you turn it on. Then you decide where your requests go.

On this device

The AI built into your phone, or a model you download. Private and works offline.

Your own model

Ollama or LM Studio on your computer or network, or a provider with your own API key.

Tija Cloud

Claude through your Tija account, with one daily allowance shared by all models. Needs sign-in.
