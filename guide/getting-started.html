Guide / Getting started

Getting started

Install Tija, take the short tour and find your way around. You can start writing straight away: no account needed.

Install Tija

  • Android
  • Windows
  • iPhone, iPad, Mac: coming soon

Get Tija for Android on Google Play. On Windows, get it from the Microsoft Store; it updates itself from there.

The welcome tour

The first time you open Tija, a short tour shows what it can do: writing, planning, the AI assistant, sync and reminders. The last step asks you to allow notifications so reminders can reach you.

After an update, a shorter tour shows what's new.

On a computer you can use the keyboard:

KeyDoes
→ / ←Next / previous step
EnterContinue
EscSkip the tour

To see it again, go to Profile → About Tija → Replay introduction.

With or without an account

Tija works fully without an account. Everything you create stays on your device.

An account is optional. It adds:

  • backup, and sync between your devices;
  • Tija Cloud for the AI assistant, and search by meaning;
  • connecting other AI apps to your notes.

Anything you made before signing in is kept and added to your account. See Account, sync & data.

Finding your way around

On a phone, the main sections are in the bar at the bottom. On a wider screen, such as a computer, they're in a sidebar on the left, which you can collapse with Collapse sidebar.

SectionWhat's there
HomeYour Scheduled Tasks and the calendar. With the AI assistant on, also Plan my day and Weekly review.
FilesAll your notes and tasks, your folders, and search.
AddStart a new note or task.
TemplatesReady-made and your own templates.
ProfileYour account, Cloud & Sync, theme and all settings.

On a computer, the sidebar also has Quick Access (All Files, Pinned, Recently Viewed, Recently Edited) and a Files & Folders tree. Right-click a task or folder for more options.

In Tija, a note becomes a task when it has a due date. So the Files section holds both, and the app often says "file/task".

Settings at a glance

Everything is in Profile:

  • Cloud & Sync: sign in, sync, change password or delete your account.
  • Ambience Mode: background effects. Turn it off for better performance.
  • Theme: System default, Light or Dark.
  • Tasks Settings, AI Assistant, Data and Storage, Notification Settings.
  • Help & Feedback to report a bug, and About Tija for release notes and the privacy policy.
