Guide / Account, sync & data

Account, sync & data

Tija saves everything on your device first. An account is optional: it backs up your work and keeps it in step across your devices.

Your profile

The first time you open Profile, you'll see No Profile Found, with two choices:

  • Create Profile sets up a profile on this device only: a first name, and optionally a last name, username, email, date of birth and gender. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Sign In logs in to an account you already have. Your synced work then downloads to this device.

Turn on cloud sync

  1. In Profile → Cloud Sync, select Enable Cloud Sync.
  2. Under Create Password, choose a password (the rules are below) and enter it again.
  3. Tick the box to agree to sync your data, then select Enable Cloud Sync.
  4. Enter the 6-digit code we email you and select Verify and Enable Sync. Didn't get it? Check your spam folder, or select Resend Code.

Cloud Sync now shows Enabled. Everything you made before signing in is kept and added to your account.

Password rules

Your password needs:

  • at least 8 characters;
  • an uppercase and a lowercase letter;
  • a number;
  • a symbol, such as ! @ # $.

The same rules apply when you change or reset it. To change it, go to Cloud Sync → Change Password. If you forgot it, select Forgot Password on the login screen and we'll email you a code.

How sync works

  • Offline first. Every change is saved on your device straight away, with or without a connection. Changes made offline are sent when you're back online.
  • In the background. While Tija is open, changes usually reach your other devices within seconds. When it's in the background, it syncs about every 15 minutes.
  • Deletes sync too. Something you delete on one device is removed from all of them.
  • Your profile syncs. Your name and profile picture stay up to date everywhere.

When two devices change the same thing

If the same note is changed on two devices before they sync, the most recent change wins, for the whole note. Changes aren't merged line by line. If one device deletes a note, the delete wins.

Working on a long note on two devices? Let one sync before you edit on the other.

Turn off sync, log out or delete your account

In Profile → Cloud Sync:

  • Disable Cloud Sync stops syncing. Your data stays on your device, and you can turn sync on again later.
  • Logout sends your latest changes first, then disconnects. Your data stays on this device and syncs again when you log back in.
  • Delete Account permanently deletes your account and everything stored with it in the cloud. You can choose whether to also erase the notes on this device. Full details.

Where your data is stored

DeviceLocation
Android, iPhone, iPadThe app's private storage. Uninstalling Tija removes it.
Windows%LOCALAPPDATA%\Tija (database, attachments and profile pictures)
MacTija's own app container in your user Library
Cloud (with an account)Our hosting provider, Supabase, in the EU (Ireland)

Downloaded AI models are stored on the device too.

On Windows, if you used an early test version that kept data in your Documents folder, Tija moved it to the new location automatically the first time you opened the new version.

Backing up

  • With an account, your synced work is your backup: sign in on any device to get it back.
  • Without one, you can export single notes as PDF or Markdown.

Data and Storage settings

Profile → Data and Storage shows how much space Tija uses, and has three clean-up actions:

  • Delete Files: deletes all your files/tasks on this device.
  • Delete Reminders: deletes all reminders.
  • Wipe All Data: erases everything Tija has stored on this device (profile, tasks, folders, templates, reminders) and signs you out.

These actions can't be undone. They aren't the way to delete your account: for that, see Delete your account.
