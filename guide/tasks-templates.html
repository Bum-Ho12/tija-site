Tasks, calendar & templates

Any note becomes a task when you give it a due date. Then it shows on the calendar, gets an urgency colour and can remind you.

Due dates and urgency

The bar at the bottom of the editor holds a task's details:

  • Due date: select the calendar chip to pick a date, and the clock to set a time. Select ✕ (or long-press the chip) to clear it.
  • Urgency (Select urgency): URGENT, HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW. New tasks start as low.
  • Folder: shows Root or the folder name. Select it to choose another folder.
  • Reminders appears once the task has a due date. See Reminders.

Tija adjusts the urgency it shows as dates pass: a task due today shows as TODAY, and one past its due date as OVERDUE.

To mark a task done, use the ⋯ menu → Mark as complete. A task needs a due date to be completed. Clearing the due date also removes its reminders.

Folders

  1. Go to Files and select New Folder (on a computer it's also in the sidebar).
  2. Enter a name and select Create.
  3. Move a task with Move to... on its card, or the folder chip in the editor. Root means no folder.

On a computer, right-click a folder in the sidebar and choose Delete Folder to remove it. The tasks inside move to Root; they're not deleted.

Finding your tasks

Files

The chips at the top filter by All, Root or a folder. The All view is split into Pinned, Recent (opened or changed in the last 7 days) and All Tasks.

On a computer, Quick Access in the sidebar opens Pinned, Recently Viewed and Recently Edited directly.

Home and the calendar

Home shows your Scheduled Tasks for the day you pick on the calendar. On a phone the calendar is a strip of days; on a wide screen there's a full month view. Colours show the urgency of each day's tasks, from Overdue to Low.

Search

  1. In Files, select the search icon (Search files/tasks).
  2. Type words from the title or content and press Enter.
  3. Narrow the results with Overdue, Today, Urgent, High, Medium or Low.

With an account and the AI assistant on, results also include Related by meaning: notes about the same thing even if they use different words. This covers synced notes only. You can also type a question and select ✨ to ask Tija about your notes.

Tasks Settings

In Profile → Tasks Settings:

  • Auto-delete Completed Tasks removes completed tasks after a set number of hours (1 to 168).
  • Hide Completed Tasks keeps your lists clean.
  • Show Only Tasks hides notes without due dates.

Auto-delete permanently removes completed tasks. It can't be undone.

Templates

A template is a ready-made starting point: a title, blocks, an urgency and a due date a set number of days ahead.

Use a template

  1. Go to Templates and select Use on a template. Or, while adding a task, select Use Template in the top bar.
  2. Change anything you like. The task is saved once you edit it.

Filter templates by category (Work, Personal, Health and more). Templates marked Bundled come with Tija; yours are marked My Template.

Make your own

  1. In Templates, select New.
  2. Fill in Template Name, a Description, an optional File/Task Title and the blocks.
  3. In the bottom bar, set the Category and the Due Days (for example 1 week).

Templates save as you go. To change or remove one of yours, use its menu → Edit or Delete.

Generate one with AI

With the AI assistant on, select ✨ Generate with AI on the Templates page, describe what you need (for example "A weekly 1:1 meeting with my manager, with agenda, wins, blockers and follow-ups") and select Generate. Then choose Open in editor to review and save it.
