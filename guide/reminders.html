Guide / Reminders

Reminders

Get a notification before a task is due, even when Tija isn't open. Reminders are created on your device and send nothing to us.

Set a reminder

  1. Give the task a due date (and a time, if it matters).
  2. Select Reminders in the bar under the editor.
  3. In Remind me before due date, tick one or more: 1 minute, 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, 1 day, 2 days or 1 week before.
  • Each option shows when it will go off. Options marked Would be in the past can't be chosen.
  • If the due date has no time, reminders count back from 8:00.
  • Change the due date and the reminders move with it. Clear it, and they're removed.
  • To cancel them all for a task, select Remove all reminders.

Reminders don't repeat yet. For a weekly task, a template makes it quick to create the next one.

Allow notifications

The welcome tour asks for permission. If you skipped it, or reminders don't arrive, go to Profile → Notification Settings. It shows All permissions granted or Some permissions need attention.

Android

  1. Select Grant Permissions, or Open App Settings.
  2. Allow Notifications.
  3. Allow Alarms & reminders (shown in Tija as Exact Alarm Permission), so reminders go off at the exact time.

Some phones delay apps to save battery. If reminders arrive late, turn off battery optimisation for Tija in your phone's battery settings.

Windows

  1. Open Windows Settings → System → Notifications.
  2. Make sure notifications are on, and on for Tija.
  3. Check that Do not disturb is off, or that your focus settings let Tija's notifications through.

Clearing notifications

Clear All Notifications in Notification Settings cancels every scheduled reminder. You'll need to set them again.

The morning brief

  • Android

A short notification each morning, titled Good morning, with how many tasks are due today and overdue and which to start with. It's worked out on your device; no AI is involved.

  • Turn it on in Profile → AI Assistant → Morning brief.
  • It arrives once a day, some time between 6:00 and 11:00.
  • If nothing is due or overdue, nothing is sent.

The Android home-screen widget

  • Android

The Tija Task Calendar Widget shows the days ahead, with today highlighted and coloured dots for each task's urgency.

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen and choose Widgets.
  2. Find Tija and drag the widget onto your home screen.
  3. Resize it: narrow shows 3 days, medium 5 days, and wide a full Monday-to-Sunday week.

Tap the widget to open Tija.
