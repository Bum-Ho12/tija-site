What's new

Release notes for each version of Tija. Your app shows its version in Profile → App Info.

0.4.0

October 2026

  • Group blocks: select blocks and choose Group. A group is a box with a name that moves as one, and its blocks can still be edited and reordered one at a time. Drag a block out of the group to take it out, or drop one among the group's blocks to add it; a Break the group here button is there if you meant to split the group instead. Drag the green strip on the group's left to move all of it. Groups.
  • Drag without getting stuck: the page now scrolls by itself when you carry a block towards the top or bottom, even into the header or toolbar, so you can reach any place in a long note. A row of blocks has its own strip on the left that moves the whole row, and the grips inside it move one block at a time. Drop just above the first block, or below the last, to put a block at the start or end. Rows and columns.
  • Pin what you watch: turn on Keep at the top while scrolling for a progress bar or result, and it stays at the top of the screen once you scroll past it. Put several widgets, such as a text, a progress bar and a figure, in a group to pin them together. If you group some that are pinned with some that aren't, Tija asks whether to pin them all or unpin them all. Widgets.
  • Easier widgets: the widget builder now saves as you go, so there is no Done button to miss. A bar or result shows what you pick from a list in plain words, such as "Checklist items ticked" or a timer's "time left", instead of a typed name like note.done. A calculation is two values and a sign, and Show a value in this text puts a value into a sentence.
  • Made for big screens: on a large window the assistant, templates, reminders, due days and the widget builder open in a side panel beside your note. Drag its edge to resize it. In Editor Settings you can turn the panel off or swap its side with the sidebar. On a tablet or computer, menus and choices open next to what you tapped, not from the bottom of the screen.
  • Selecting blocks: what you can do to the selected blocks now appears right beside them, instead of in a bar over the page. On a computer use Esc to finish, Ctrl/⌘ + C, D and A, and Delete. Select blocks.
  • Going back: swipe from the left edge on iPhone and iPad, use Android's back gesture, or on a wide screen click any step of the path above your note (such as Files or a folder's name). Finding your way around.
  • Keyboard: tap empty space, or drag the page, to close the keyboard; there is also a hide keyboard button above it.
  • Fixed: a highlighted result no longer shows through the search field above a list; the pinned bar is centered and easy to tell apart from your note; and pages no longer show an error as they open.

0.3.0

October 2026

  • Write a whole note with AI: in Add, open the ✨ menu and choose Write a note. Describe what you need, then Use this or Edit request. Ask for changes in the box under the result, such as "make it shorter". Create template from this note keeps a note's structure and drops the details. How it works.
  • Fix or rewrite part of a note: long-press a block (right-click on a computer) to select it, or select the whole note, then use Edit with AI: fix spelling, improve, shorten, expand, change the tone, simplify, turn into a checklist, translate, or ask for any change. You see what changes, in red and green, before you choose Replace. Selecting blocks.
  • Blocks side by side: drag a block by its grip onto the left or right edge of another to make a row of up to three columns. Drop on the upper or lower half to put it above or below. Drag the divider to resize the columns. On a small screen, columns that would be too narrow stack. Exports keep the layout. Rows and columns.
  • Interactive widgets: add counters, buttons, switches, inputs, choices, progress bars, results, tabs and bar charts that work together, with / → Interactive widget. Build one piece by piece, paste it, or describe it to the assistant. Widgets are plain data: they can't run code, open links or reach the network. Widgets.
  • Widgets that read your note: a progress bar can follow the ticked items in your note, or how far you have read, and text can show word counts and the time.
  • Timers that go off: countdowns with rounds and rest, and stopwatches. When one ends it can flash the screen green, sound a full-screen alarm until you stop it, vibrate, or send a notification that works with Tija closed. A timer keeps running when you leave the note. Calm timer alerts in Editor Settings swaps the pulsing flash for a steady tint. Timers.
  • Images and swipes: resize and align images, and fill the space beside a narrowed image with the next block. On a touch screen, swipe a block to delete, select or duplicate it (you choose in Editor Settings). Undo and redo work in every note and template.
  • Fixed: sync no longer gets stuck on a folder that was deleted on another device or a task stored twice.

0.2.6

October 2026

  • Sign out and pause are different now: sign out unlinks this device from your account and keeps your notes. A new Sync with cloud switch pauses syncing while you stay signed in, and nothing leaves your device until you turn it back on.
  • Signing back in is quicker: Tija remembers your account, so it shows "Sign in as you@example.com" and asks only for your password. Password managers can fill it in.
  • No more being signed out offline: opening Tija without a connection no longer signs you out. If your session really does end, you get a "Sign in" message and your notes stay put.
  • Switching accounts asks first: if a different account signs in, you choose whether to add the notes on this device to it or to replace them.
  • Status bar on computers: a slim bar along the bottom shows your sync status and recent messages, in the same color as the sidebar. Important problems appear as a banner you can hide.
  • Messages on phones: offline, a slow sync or a failed sync now show in a thin strip at the top, and important messages become a banner. Tap it for details and what to do.
  • Reminder warnings: if you have reminders but Tija isn't allowed to alert you, a banner tells you and takes you to the setting. If syncing keeps failing for a day, you get one quiet notification.
  • Fixed: messages no longer pile up behind one another, and the bottom bar stays centered on devices with a navigation bar.

0.2.5

October 2026

  • Rename and delete folders: long-press a folder (right-click on a computer) to rename or delete it.
  • Deleting a folder that has files: choose to move its files to another folder, or delete them together with the folder.
  • Folders in Recently deleted: a deleted folder stays for 30 days in Profile → Data and Storage → Recently deleted, with the files deleted alongside it in a dropdown. Restore the folder to bring everything back, or restore or delete each file on its own. A file restored on its own goes to the root while its folder is still deleted.
  • Search everything: find screens, settings, folders, templates and files from one search. Press Ctrl K (⌘K on a Mac), use the search box at the top of the sidebar, or tap the search button on Home. Picking a setting takes you to it.
  • Search in Files: results now appear as you type, without pressing Enter, and no longer flicker.
  • A better "/" menu: it opens right at the slash and follows it as you scroll, and moves above the slash when there isn't room below. On a phone it becomes a tray just above the keyboard, so the keyboard can't cover it. It now also opens after a line break, accepts spaces (/heading 1), and Tab picks the highlighted item. Items are grouped, and text you typed after a "/" is kept if you tap away.
  • Clearer tips: the short tips that point out parts of a screen now spotlight what they describe, show how many there are, and have Back and Skip all. They follow the screen as it scrolls and now appear on computers too. Profile → About Tija → Replay tips shows them again.
  • Fixed: on a computer, going back from Add crashed the app, and opening Files could jump straight to Add.
  • Fixed: the storage bar and the version number in About and Profile didn't show on Windows. They now work on Windows, macOS and Linux.

0.2.4

October 2026

  • Privacy choices: Tija can send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to help fix problems. They are off until you say yes, and only for people aged 16 or older. Change your answer any time in Profile → Privacy. Your tasks, notes, files, reminders and AI prompts are never part of them, and they aren't linked to your account. The Windows version doesn't send them at all. Details in the privacy policy.
  • Terms of Use: read them in the app under Profile → About, or on this site.

0.2.3

October 2026

  • Cloud & Sync has its own screen: Profile now shows a Cloud & Sync summary that opens sign-in, sign-out, password and account actions.
  • Recently deleted: deleted notes, folders and templates stay for 30 days in Profile → Data and Storage → Recently deleted. Restore them (on all your devices), delete them forever or empty the list. Delete Files now works the same way and syncs.
  • Account deletion takes 7 days: Delete Account schedules the deletion and signs you out everywhere. Sign in again within 7 days and choose Keep my account to cancel it. How it works.
  • Tester codes: if you were given a code, enter it when you create your account or in Profile → AI Assistant for a bigger daily AI allowance for a limited time.
  • Fixed: a Force Full Sync button that did nothing was removed.

0.2.2

October 2026

  • One daily AI allowance: with Tija Cloud, Haiku, Sonnet and Opus now share a single daily allowance (used alone, 15, 10 or 5 requests). Heavier models use more of it, and when it runs out no model works until midnight UTC.

0.2.1

October 2026 · First Windows release

  • Tija on Windows: the full app on your PC, with a sidebar, wider layouts and notifications for reminders.
  • Your data in the right place on Windows: notes, attachments and profile pictures are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Tija. Data from earlier test builds is moved there from Documents automatically on first launch.
  • Welcome tour: a short introduction when you first open Tija, and a look at what's new after an update. Replay it any time from Profile → About Tija → Replay introduction. On a computer, use ← → to move, Enter to continue and Esc to skip.
  • Calendar month view and layouts that use the space on bigger screens.
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile → Cloud & Sync → Delete Account. How it works.
  • Report an AI response with the ⚑ button on any AI result.
  • Privacy policy link in Profile → About Tija.

0.2.0

October 2026

Tija 0.2.0 adds an optional AI assistant and brings your work to the cloud, so your tasks, files and templates go wherever you do.

AI assistant

  1. Quick Capture: type a task the way you'd say it and Tija fills in the title, due date and priority.
  2. Plan Your Day: get a suggested order for what's due, and move things to later.
  3. Writing Tools: improve writing, summarize, continue writing, break a block into steps or pull out action items.
  4. Text from Image: turn a photo or screenshot into text.
  5. Link Summaries: get the gist of a saved link without leaving Tija.
  6. Generate a Template: describe what you need and open the result in the editor.
  7. Suggest Details: get a suggested priority and folder for a task.
  8. Ask Your Notes: ask a question and get answers from your notes, or search by meaning.

The assistant is off until you turn it on, and you review every suggestion before it's saved.

Your AI, your choice

  • On This Device: use the AI built into your phone, or download a model that runs entirely offline.
  • Your Own Model: connect Ollama, LM Studio or a provider with your own API key.
  • Tija Cloud: use Claude through your Tija account, with a daily allowance.
  • Connect Other AI Apps: let Claude Desktop, Claude Code or Cursor read your notes and, if you allow it, change them.

Accounts & sync

  • Keep your local work: anything you created before signing in is added to your account when you link it.
  • Offline first: everything is saved on your device first and synced when you're back online.
  • Deletes sync too: items you remove on one device are removed everywhere.
  • Profile sync: your name and profile picture stay up to date across devices.
  • Stronger password rules: at least 8 characters with upper and lower case letters, a number and a symbol.

Editor & tasks

  • Formatting bar: bold, italic, strikethrough, highlight, inline code and links as you type.
  • Radio buttons: add single-choice options to tasks and templates; selecting one clears the others.
  • PDF export: radio buttons and checkboxes export with their selected state.
  • Morning brief: a daily notification of what's due, worked out on your device.
  • More views: browse pinned, recently viewed and recently edited tasks.

Fixes & polish

  • The Ambience mode toggle now works in both light and dark themes.
  • Improved layout of the bottom editor and page wrapper.
  • Bottom sheets and the sidebar now render touch effects and rounded corners correctly.
  • Profile pictures show consistently across navigation and profile screens.
