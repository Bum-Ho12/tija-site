What's new

Release notes for each version of Tija. Your app shows its version in Profile → App Info.

0.2.5

October 2026

  • Rename and delete folders: long-press a folder (right-click on a computer) to rename or delete it.
  • Deleting a folder that has files: choose to move its files to another folder, or delete them together with the folder.
  • Folders in Recently deleted: a deleted folder stays for 30 days in Profile → Data and Storage → Recently deleted, with the files deleted alongside it in a dropdown. Restore the folder to bring everything back, or restore or delete each file on its own. A file restored on its own goes to the root while its folder is still deleted.
  • Search everything: find screens, settings, folders, templates and files from one search. Press Ctrl K (⌘K on a Mac), use the search box at the top of the sidebar, or tap the search button on Home. Picking a setting takes you to it.
  • Search in Files: results now appear as you type, without pressing Enter, and no longer flicker.
  • Fixed: the storage bar and the version number in About and Profile didn't show on Windows. They now work on Windows, macOS and Linux.

0.2.4

October 2026

  • Privacy choices: Tija can send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to help fix problems. They are off until you say yes, and only for people aged 16 or older. Change your answer any time in Profile → Privacy. Your tasks, notes, files, reminders and AI prompts are never part of them, and they aren't linked to your account. The Windows version doesn't send them at all. Details in the privacy policy.
  • Terms of Use: read them in the app under Profile → About, or on this site.

0.2.3

October 2026

  • Cloud & Sync has its own screen: Profile now shows a Cloud & Sync summary that opens sign-in, sign-out, password and account actions.
  • Recently deleted: deleted notes, folders and templates stay for 30 days in Profile → Data and Storage → Recently deleted. Restore them (on all your devices), delete them forever or empty the list. Delete Files now works the same way and syncs.
  • Account deletion takes 7 days: Delete Account schedules the deletion and signs you out everywhere. Sign in again within 7 days and choose Keep my account to cancel it. How it works.
  • Tester codes: if you were given a code, enter it when you create your account or in Profile → AI Assistant for a bigger daily AI allowance for a limited time.
  • Fixed: a Force Full Sync button that did nothing was removed.

0.2.2

October 2026

  • One daily AI allowance: with Tija Cloud, Haiku, Sonnet and Opus now share a single daily allowance (used alone, 15, 10 or 5 requests). Heavier models use more of it, and when it runs out no model works until midnight UTC.

0.2.1

October 2026 · First Windows release

  • Tija on Windows: the full app on your PC, with a sidebar, wider layouts and notifications for reminders.
  • Your data in the right place on Windows: notes, attachments and profile pictures are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Tija. Data from earlier test builds is moved there from Documents automatically on first launch.
  • Welcome tour: a short introduction when you first open Tija, and a look at what's new after an update. Replay it any time from Profile → About Tija → Replay introduction. On a computer, use ← → to move, Enter to continue and Esc to skip.
  • Calendar month view and layouts that use the space on bigger screens.
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile → Cloud & Sync → Delete Account. How it works.
  • Report an AI response with the ⚑ button on any AI result.
  • Privacy policy link in Profile → About Tija.

0.2.0

October 2026

Tija 0.2.0 adds an optional AI assistant and brings your work to the cloud, so your tasks, files and templates go wherever you do.

AI assistant

  1. Quick Capture: type a task the way you'd say it and Tija fills in the title, due date and priority.
  2. Plan Your Day: get a suggested order for what's due, and move things to later.
  3. Writing Tools: improve writing, summarize, continue writing, break a block into steps or pull out action items.
  4. Text from Image: turn a photo or screenshot into text.
  5. Link Summaries: get the gist of a saved link without leaving Tija.
  6. Generate a Template: describe what you need and open the result in the editor.
  7. Suggest Details: get a suggested priority and folder for a task.
  8. Ask Your Notes: ask a question and get answers from your notes, or search by meaning.

The assistant is off until you turn it on, and you review every suggestion before it's saved.

Your AI, your choice

  • On This Device: use the AI built into your phone, or download a model that runs entirely offline.
  • Your Own Model: connect Ollama, LM Studio or a provider with your own API key.
  • Tija Cloud: use Claude through your Tija account, with a daily allowance.
  • Connect Other AI Apps: let Claude Desktop, Claude Code or Cursor read your notes and, if you allow it, change them.

Accounts & sync

  • Keep your local work: anything you created before signing in is added to your account when you link it.
  • Offline first: everything is saved on your device first and synced when you're back online.
  • Deletes sync too: items you remove on one device are removed everywhere.
  • Profile sync: your name and profile picture stay up to date across devices.
  • Stronger password rules: at least 8 characters with upper and lower case letters, a number and a symbol.

Editor & tasks

  • Formatting bar: bold, italic, strikethrough, highlight, inline code and links as you type.
  • Radio buttons: add single-choice options to tasks and templates; selecting one clears the others.
  • PDF export: radio buttons and checkboxes export with their selected state.
  • Morning brief: a daily notification of what's due, worked out on your device.
  • More views: browse pinned, recently viewed and recently edited tasks.

Fixes & polish

  • The Ambience mode toggle now works in both light and dark themes.
  • Improved layout of the bottom editor and page wrapper.
  • Bottom sheets and the sidebar now render touch effects and rounded corners correctly.
  • Profile pictures show consistently across navigation and profile screens.
