Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Tija works fully without an account, and everything stays on your device. An account adds backup and sync between devices, and it's needed for Tija Cloud AI, search by meaning and connecting other AI apps. About accounts and sync.

Is my data private?

Without an account, your notes never leave your device. With an account, they're stored with our hosting provider in the EU so they can sync. We don't sell data or show ads, and the AI assistant is off until you turn it on. Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports are optional and off until you say yes.

Read the full privacy policy.

What are usage statistics and crash reports, and how do I turn them off?

Tija asks once whether it may send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (which features are used, and when the app fails). Nothing is sent unless you say yes, and they never include your tasks, notes, files, name or email.

To change your answer, open Profile → Privacy and switch Share usage and crash reports on or off. The Windows version doesn't send them. Details are in the privacy policy.

Why didn't my reminder go off?
  1. Check that the task still has a due date. Clearing the due date also removes its reminders.
  2. Go to Profile → Notification Settings. If it says Some permissions need attention, select Grant Permissions or Open App Settings.
  3. Android: allow both Notification Permission and Exact Alarm Permission (in system settings this is “Alarms & reminders”). If reminders still arrive late, turn off battery optimisation for Tija in your phone's settings.
  4. Windows: in Settings → System → Notifications, make sure notifications are on for Tija and that Do not disturb is off.

Selecting Clear All Notifications cancels every scheduled reminder, so they need to be set again. More about reminders.

My changes aren't showing on my other device
  1. On both devices, open Profile and check that Cloud & Sync says Enabled and that you're signed in to the same account.
  2. Make sure both devices are online. Changes made offline are kept and sent when the device reconnects.
  3. Open Tija on the other device. Changes usually arrive within seconds while the app is open; in the background, sync runs about every 15 minutes.

If two devices change the same note, the most recent change wins. How sync handles conflicts.

I can't see the AI features

The assistant is off by default. Turn on Use the AI assistant in Profile → AI Assistant. To use Tija Cloud you also need to be signed in. Set up the assistant.

I've reached my daily AI limit

With Tija Cloud, every account has one daily allowance shared by all models, and heavier models use more of it. Used alone it gives 15 requests with Haiku, 10 with Sonnet or 5 with Opus. It resets at midnight UTC. Search by meaning has its own allowance.

Switching to another Claude model won't help, because they share the allowance. Until midnight UTC, switch to Your own model or On this device in Profile → AI Assistant, which have no Tija limit. If you were given a tester code, enter it there for a bigger allowance.

The on-device model won't download or run
  • Check you have enough free space: about 0.5 GB, 1.3 GB or 2.7 GB depending on the model, plus some to spare.
  • Use a stable connection. You can cancel a download and start it again, or delete a model and download it again.
  • Downloaded models need a 64-bit Android phone. On older 32-bit phones they can't run.
  • If the phone has little memory, try Qwen3.5 0.8B.
  • Select Try it to test the model after it downloads.
How do I move to a new phone or computer?
  1. On your old device, set up an account if you haven't: Profile → Cloud & Sync → Enable Cloud Sync. Everything you've made is added to the account.
  2. On the new device, install Tija, go to Profile and select Sign In (or Login), using the same email and password.

Without an account there's no way to move everything at once, but you can export single notes as PDF or Markdown.

I forgot my password

On the login screen, select Forgot Password, enter your email address and select Send OTP. Enter the 6-digit code from the email and choose a new password.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Cloud & Sync → Delete Account. The account is deleted after 7 days, and signing in again within that time keeps it. If you no longer have the app, you can ask us by email. Full instructions.

I deleted something by mistake

Notes, folders and templates stay in Profile → Data and Storage → Recently deleted for 30 days. Select Restore to bring one back (on all your devices, if you sync). After 30 days it's removed for good.

Deleted reminders, and anything erased with Wipe All Data, can't be restored. More about Recently deleted.

How do I use a tester code?

Enter it when you create your account (select Have a tester code?; it's applied once you confirm your email), or later in Profile → AI Assistant under Have a tester code?. It gives you a bigger daily AI allowance until it ends, and then your account goes back to the normal allowance. A code works for a limited time and number of people, and too many wrong tries pause new attempts for an hour.
